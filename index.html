BGBF Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

BGBF ("Board Game's Best Friend") is a board game companion application designed to help users keep track of scores and other game-related information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you use BGBF.

Information You Provide

BGBF does not require you to create an account in order to use the app.

Information entered into the app for board game sessions, such as player names, scores, notes, or other game-related information, is used to provide the app's functionality.

Age Information

BGBF asks users to provide their date of birth through a neutral age screen. The date of birth is stored locally on the user's device and is used to determine an age category for privacy and advertising purposes.

BGBF does not intentionally transmit the date of birth provided through this screen to Google AdMob or other third parties. Advertising features use the resulting age classification rather than the user's date of birth.

For users classified as minors based on the date of birth they provide, BGBF applies restricted advertising settings, including non-personalized advertising and age-appropriate advertising restrictions.

This age classification is based on the date of birth supplied by the user and should not be interpreted as independent verification of the user's legal age under the laws of every jurisdiction.

Advertising

BGBF uses Google AdMob, a mobile advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information from your device in order to provide, measure, and improve advertising. Depending on your location, age classification, device settings, consent choices, and applicable law, this information may include device or other identifiers, IP address or approximate location information, interactions with the app or advertisements, and diagnostic information.

Information processed through Google Mobile Ads may be used for purposes such as advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance, subject to Google's policies, applicable law, and the privacy settings applied by BGBF.

The information collected and processed by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, adult users may be presented with Google's consent and privacy choices before certain advertising technologies are used.

The privacy and advertising choices available to users may depend on their location, age classification, and applicable privacy laws.

Where required by Google's consent platform, BGBF provides a "Privacy options" entry in the app that allows eligible users to review or update available privacy choices.

Third-Party Services

BGBF uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies.

The primary third-party service currently used by BGBF is Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads.

Children's Privacy

BGBF does not require users to create an account to use its board game scoring and companion features.

BGBF uses the locally stored date of birth described above to apply age-appropriate privacy and advertising settings. For users classified as minors, BGBF applies restricted advertising treatment and does not intentionally request personalized advertising.

Third-party advertising services may process information as described in their respective privacy policies, subject to the age-related settings applied by BGBF, applicable laws, and platform requirements.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information associated with the operation of BGBF. Data transmitted through supported third-party services is protected in transit according to the security practices of those services. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to BGBF, third-party services, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised when appropriate.
