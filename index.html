BGBF Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

BGBF ("Board Game's Best Friend") is a board game companion application designed to help users keep track of scores and other game-related information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you use BGBF.

Information You Provide

BGBF does not require you to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the app.

Information entered into the app for board game sessions, such as player names, scores, notes, or other game-related information, is used to provide the app's functionality.

Advertising

BGBF uses Google AdMob, a mobile advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information from your device in order to provide, measure, and improve advertising. Depending on your location, device settings, consent choices, and applicable law, this information may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, general location information, interactions with advertisements, and diagnostic information.

The information collected and processed by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, BGBF may ask for your consent before certain advertising technologies are used.

The privacy and advertising choices available to you may depend on your location and applicable privacy laws.

Third-Party Services

BGBF uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies.

The primary third-party service currently used by BGBF is Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads.

Children's Privacy

BGBF is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. The app does not require users to create accounts or provide personal information to use its board game scoring features.

Advertising services used by the app may process information as described in their respective privacy policies and in accordance with applicable laws and platform requirements.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information associated with the operation of BGBF. However, no electronic transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to BGBF, third-party services, or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised when appropriate.
